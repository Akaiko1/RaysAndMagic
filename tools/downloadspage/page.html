First launch

Each archive holds the game and the map editor (RaysAndMagicMapViewer).

macOS 12 or newer

One download runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Keep both apps together, in the unpacked folder or in Applications: the map editor reads the game's files.

The apps are signed ad hoc and not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch of each one:

  1. Unzip the archive and open RaysAndMagic.app. When macOS refuses, choose Done.
  2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down and press Open Anyway next to RaysAndMagic.
  3. Confirm once more. Later launches open normally.

On macOS 12 to 14 you can instead Control-click the app and choose Open. Or clear the download flag in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine RaysAndMagic.app

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

The executables are not code-signed, so SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway.

Saves

macOS keeps saves in ~/Library/Application Support/RaysAndMagic, so a new build finds them. On Windows they live in the saves folder next to RaysAndMagic.exe: copy that folder over when you unpack an update.
