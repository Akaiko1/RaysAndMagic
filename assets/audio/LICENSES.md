# Audio asset sources

All selected third-party source sound effects are licensed under Creative
Commons Zero (CC0). The files shipped by the game are selected, shortened, or
layered where needed, loudness-normalized, converted to 48 kHz stereo, and
encoded as Ogg Vorbis. Attribution is not required, but this source record is
kept for provenance.
