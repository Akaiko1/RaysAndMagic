Alagard
=======

Author: Hewett Tsoi
Source: https://www.dafont.com/alagard.font
Terms:  "100% Free". The author's note: "Give credit if used. That's it."

A gothic bitmap font for fantasy games, drawn on a 16px grid. The game draws
it at its design size (see display.fonts in config.yaml) and credits the
author in Settings > Display.
