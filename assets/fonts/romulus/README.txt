Romulus
=======

Author: Hewett Tsoi
Source: https://www.dafont.com/romulus.font
Terms:  "100% Free". The author's note: "Credit is appreciated."

A serifed bitmap font made for games, drawn on a 16px grid. The game draws it
at its design size (see display.fonts in config.yaml) and credits the author
in Settings > Display.
