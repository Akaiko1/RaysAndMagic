STYLE FOR ALL IMAGES: detailed anime-fantasy pixel-art painting, crisp deliberate pixel clusters, dark contours, controlled colour blocks, mature proportions, warm lantern or firelight against cool night shadows.

When the player's party appears, show four adventurers only from behind, hooded or helmeted, small in the foreground, their faces never visible.
